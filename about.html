About PlagiGuard

We exist to reduce manual effort in academic integrity workflows while presenting similarity information clearly and transparently.

Project Purpose

PlagiGuard was created to help instructors and academic teams maintain academic integrity without spending excessive time manually comparing student submissions. Our goal is to surface potentially similar assignments in a clean, organized, and actionable way.

Who Is PlagiGuard For?

Instructors

Quickly identify which submissions require deeper review instead of reading every assignment manually.

Teaching Assistants

Organize and prioritize flagged pairs efficiently across large cohorts.

Academic Teams

Gain clear visibility into similarity trends across courses and semesters.

Project Value

Faster Review

Significantly reduces the amount of manual scanning required.

Clear Presentation

Makes suspicious submissions easy to identify and act upon.

Simple Workflow

Keeps all submission and review information organized in one place.
