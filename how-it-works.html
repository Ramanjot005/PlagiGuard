HOW IT WORKS

How the scoring works

The complete similarity flow is written in vanilla JavaScript and runs in the browser.

1
Minimum words

Reject very short submissions before analysis.

2
Tokenization

Lowercase text and remove punctuation.

3
N-grams

Make overlapping groups of N consecutive words.

4
Jaccard

Intersection size divided by union size, shown as a percentage.

5
Flag

Compare the score with the instructor threshold.

6
Review

Instructor reads, marks and gives feedback.

Access rules

Guests can access Home and How It Works. Student and instructor dashboards require login. Signup creates the chosen role and the application redirects to the correct dashboard.

Current limitations

This version intentionally uses localStorage/sessionStorage and has no REST API, JWT, database, or external plagiarism API. It is designed as a vanilla-JavaScript classroom demonstration.
