Academic integrity workspace

Built to make assignment review clearer and fairer.

PlagiGuard gives students a simple place to submit work and understand their result, while instructors get the controls they need to create assignments, inspect similarity, mark submissions and give feedback.

🎓

For students

See available assignments, submit work, check word-count rules, view your similarity status and keep up with instructor marks and feedback.

🧑‍🏫

For instructors

Create the next assignment in seconds, review every submission, configure the scoring rules, inspect matching n-grams and record marks.

🔬

Transparent scoring

The browser calculates tokenization, configurable n-grams and Jaccard similarity so the core scoring logic is visible rather than hidden behind an external plagiarism API.

OUR APPROACH

Similarity is a signal, not a verdict.

A high similarity score helps an instructor decide what deserves a closer look. It does not automatically prove academic misconduct. PlagiGuard keeps the human review step visible by showing the score, top match and shared n-grams alongside the original submission.
