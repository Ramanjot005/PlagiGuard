Phase 1 pipeline

From pasted text to a ranked suspicion list

Seven steps. Each one is a small, testable piece of vanilla JavaScript.

1

Collect the submission

The student pastes the assignment into a textarea. Before anything is stored, the form counts the words and refuses anything under the minimum limit, because a two-line answer cannot be compared fairly.

textarea trim() split(/\s+/) min 50 words
2

Normalise the text

Everything is lower-cased, punctuation is removed and extra spaces are collapsed. This way "Energy," and "energy" are treated as the same word, so simple formatting tricks do not hide a copy.

toLowerCase() replace() regex
3

Tokenise into words

The clean text is split into an array of single words. This array is the raw material for the next step.

split() filter() Array
4

Build n-grams (shingles)

A sliding window of n words moves across the array. With n = 3, the sentence "solar power is very clean" becomes "solar power is", "power is very", "is very clean". Word order is preserved, which is exactly what catches copied sentences.

for loop slice(i, i+n) join(" ") Set
5

Compare every pair with Jaccard

For two submissions A and B we count the n-grams that appear in both (intersection) and divide by the total number of unique n-grams (union). Two nested loops cover all n × (n-1) / 2 pairs.

nested for Set.has() intersection / union
6

Save and rank

Submissions and their scores are kept in the browser's LocalStorage so a page refresh does not lose the work. The pair list is then sorted from the highest score down.

JSON.stringify() JSON.parse() sort()
7

Render the matrix and the list

The DOM layer builds a colour-coded grid plus a ranked table. Green means safe, red means the pair needs a human review. This is the only step that touches the screen.

createElement() innerHTML classList

Worked example (n = 3)

Submission A

solar power is very clean

Submission B

solar power is very cheap

3-grams of A

solar power is power is very is very clean

3-grams of B

solar power is power is very is very cheap
Shared = 2  ·  Total unique = 4
Jaccard = 2 / 4 = 0.50 → 50%

Score bands

Safe 0% – 34%
Low 35% – 59%
Review 60% – 74%
High risk 75% – 100%

Choosing n

A small n (2) catches reworded text but produces many false alarms. A large n (5) is very strict and misses light edits. We use n = 3 as the balanced default.
